# BNP Paribas: Mission, Purpose & Core Values

## Mission Statement

BNP Paribas's mission is to contribute to a responsible and sustainable economy by financing and advising clients according to the highest ethical standards. ([group.bnpparibas](https://group.bnpparibas/en/group/about-us/company-purpose?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Corporate Purpose

BNP Paribas aims to drive change toward sustainable finance and human-centered banking. As "the bank for a changing world," it is committed to leadership, innovation, service, and making a positive global impact. ([usa.bnpparibas](https://usa.bnpparibas/en/homepage/about-us/the-bnp-paribas-purpose/?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Core Values

BNP Paribas has defined four core values: responsiveness, creativity, commitment, and ambition. ([cardifpinnacle.com](https://www.cardifpinnacle.com/pdf/hrd_values.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

Additionally, the bank emphasizes stability, expertise, responsibility, and fostering a good working environment. ([comparably.com](https://www.comparably.com/companies/bnp-paribas/mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Sustainability and Social Responsibility

BNP Paribas is committed to making a sustainable and inclusive contribution to the environmental, societal, technological, and economic challenges of our time. ([group.bnpparibas](https://group.bnpparibas/en/our-commitments?utm_source=chatgpt.com))

## Strategic Vision

The bank aspires to be the leading European bank with a global reach, the preferred long-term partner to its clients, and a contributor to responsible and sustainable global development. ([comparably.com](https://www.comparably.com/companies/bnp-paribas/mission?utm_source=chatgpt.com))
